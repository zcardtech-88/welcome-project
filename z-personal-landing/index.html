FAQ

Simple answers.

What is Z Card?

Z Card is a digital identity platform that lets you share your professional information, links and contact options through one smart card.

Is Z Personal free?

Yes. The personal card experience is designed to be free to create and easy to share.

Can I share my card on WhatsApp and social media?

Yes. The card is built around a shareable link, QR code and native device sharing.

Can I edit my card later?

Yes in the intended product flow. This demo keeps editing local until the real account API is connected.

How do I save my card in the app?

The intended flow is phone verification followed by attaching the unclaimed card to the authenticated Z Card account. This demo exposes the integration point without faking authentication.
